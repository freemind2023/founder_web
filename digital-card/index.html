About

Sanmit Shah is an educator and entrepreneur with 19+ years of experience. His journey began with a simple belief that education should prepare students for real careers, not just exams — leading him to build Practical EduSkills, focused on practical, industry-ready learning that has transformed 2000+ students so far.

During COVID-19, he received two awards for extraordinary contribution to education, including the Yuva Warrior Award, presented by the Hon'ble Home Minister of Maharashtra.
